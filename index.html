Health Tracker Privacy Policy

Published by monkeytonik · Effective 2 November 2026

About the app

Health Tracker is a personal health and nutrition app. It is in private testing and is used only by its developer. It is not available to the public, and some of the features described here are still being built.

What the app handles, and why

  • Account details: an email address and password, so you can sign in. The password is stored only in scrambled (hashed) form.
  • Food log, body measurements and weight: to track nutrition and progress over time. Each entry is saved with its time and your phone’s time zone, so entries stay on the right day when you travel.
  • Targets and plans: your nutrition targets, goal weight, saved foods and dinner rotation, to plan meals and measure progress.
  • Whoop data (recovery, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, sleep, strain, energy burned, workouts and body measurements): to show recovery and training alongside nutrition.
  • Apple Health data: to bring in health readings you choose to share.
  • Meal photos: to estimate what is on the plate and its nutrition.
  • Chat messages: to answer your questions.
  • Notes for Claude: a short profile and standing notes you write yourself, so Claude’s replies fit you.

Where it is stored

Data the app saves is stored with Supabase in Frankfurt, Germany. Each account can see only its own data, and the database itself enforces this.

Supabase also keeps standard technical records, such as sign-in times and IP addresses, for security.

Meal photos and chat messages are not saved on the app’s servers. They are sent to Anthropic to produce a reply, and the app shows the reply on screen. Most Whoop data is not saved either: it is fetched from Whoop each time you view it. The exception is each day’s energy burned, which is saved with that day’s log to track your energy balance. The app saves only the sign-in tokens Whoop issues, so you stay connected, and those tokens stay on the server, never on your phone.

Who processes it

  • Supabase hosts the app’s database, sign-in and server functions.
  • Anthropic receives meal photos and chat messages, together with your phone’s current date, time and time zone and the parts of your log needed to answer them (today’s items and day type, targets, saved foods, dinner rotation, your notes for Claude and the last 30 days of logged foods), to generate replies with its Claude AI models.
  • Whoop and Apple are sources of data, and only when you connect them.

Read-only access to Whoop and Apple Health

The app asks Whoop and Apple Health only for permission to read data. It cannot change or delete anything in your Whoop or Apple Health account.

What the app does not do

Your data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shown to other users. The app contains no advertising or tracking tools.

Deleting your data

This is what is possible today:

  • Whoop: the app’s Disconnect Whoop button deletes the stored Whoop sign-in tokens and asks Whoop to cancel the app’s access. Each day’s energy burned stays in your log as part of your energy balance; no other Whoop data is stored.
  • Notes for Claude: you can change or clear them at any time on the Notes screen.
  • Your account: while the app is in private testing, accounts are deleted by the developer. Deleting an account also deletes everything linked to it in the app’s database.
  • Meal photos and chat messages: the app does not store them, so there is nothing for it to delete. Anthropic processes them under its commercial terms, which do not allow them to be used to train its AI models.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
